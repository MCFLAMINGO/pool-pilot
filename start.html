Liquidity first — read before you pay

A token with no seed liquidity cannot be bought or sold meaningfully. Serious floor: plan ~$5k–$15k concentrated near spot on your home chain (more if you want $500 buys to feel okay). Below ~$1k is demo-only.

Seed means your start money becomes 50/50 ETH + token in the pool — you’re buying both sides of the book, not a lottery ticket. Desk fees ($50 MCFL) are for tooling; they are not your liquidity.
