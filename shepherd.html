Shepherd — a bodyguard for your launch

Humans lose early hours to bots. Shepherd rides with your pool for a short window and follows three plain rules you pick. You stay the issuer. Funds for defense stay in your guard purse.

What it does

  • Fair open — early buys can’t be giant one-wallet snipes (soft cap / cooldown intent).
  • Sniper soak — if someone dumps hard into thin air, Shepherd plans a temporary sell wall from your guard purse.
  • Floor nurse — if the book gaps down empty, Shepherd plans a narrow buy wall so the next human isn’t catching a knife alone.

Risks — read once

  • Shepherd cannot stop all bots, rugs, or bad tokens. It reduces chaos; it does not guarantee price.
  • Your guard purse can lose money defending the book (same as LP risk / impermanent loss).
  • Thin pools still move a lot. Shepherd is not insurance.
  • You remain the issuer. Pool Pilot never custodies your keys.
  • v1 arms rules + receipt. Planned moves still need your wallet when a defense fires.
