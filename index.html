Why you can trust this

Non-custodial, always. Every transaction signs in your own wallet. Pool Pilot never holds your tokens and has no permission to move your liquidity.

Preview before you pay. Every move shows you the exact transactions before anything is sent. You pay to execute a plan you have already seen — never to find out.

Your liquidity lives in Uniswap, not in us. Moves call Uniswap v3's own audited position manager directly. There is no Pool Pilot contract holding funds.

Fees are paid in MCFL — a verified LayerZero OFT token — and every payment is a public on-chain receipt. ETH fees never swap: they are deposited as buy-side liquidity in the MCFL pool, so every payment makes the book deeper, not the price wilder.

Hardened against the usual tricks. Token names are stripped of markup, the page runs under a strict content policy that blocks any injected script, the one external library is pinned by cryptographic hash, and every deposit carries an on-chain minimum so a front-runner cannot skew it — the transaction reverts instead.
